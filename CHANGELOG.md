# GuildSuggestion 0.3.2

- Inviting needs no typing: Invite puts the player's name in chat as a link and highlights Blizzard's Invite Member button. Click it, Shift-click the name (the game fills it in, special characters and all) and Accept. WoW only lets its own window send guild invites, so this is the shortest way it allows.
- The suggestion is marked invited once the game confirms the invite went out.
- Log tab for members who can invite, with every suggestion, invite, decline and removal, and when it happened.
