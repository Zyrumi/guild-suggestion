# GuildSuggestion 0.3.1

- Fixed: Invite no longer tries to send the guild invite itself (WoW only lets its own window do that, so it was blocked). Invite now shows the player's name ready to copy at the bottom of the To decide page and highlights Blizzard's Invite Member button: Ctrl+C, click Invite Member, Ctrl+V, Accept. No typing of names with special characters.
- The suggestion is marked invited once the game confirms the invite went out.
- New Log tab for members who can invite, with every suggestion, invite, decline and removal, and when it happened.
