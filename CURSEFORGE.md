# CurseForge project text

## Summary (the one line under the name)

Let guild members suggest players to invite, with a short reason, while only the ranks that can invite decide.

## Description (paste into the description editor, Markdown)

**GuildSuggestion** is for World of Warcraft: Forever guilds that keep invite rights to a few ranks. Being careful about who joins shouldn't mean members have no say. With this addon, any member can suggest a player and say why, and the members who can invite make the call.

### How it works

Open the guild window and pick the **Guild suggestions** tab under the game's own tabs. Type the player's name and a short reason, then click **Send suggestion**. You can also right-click a player (target frame, party, raid or a name in chat) and pick **Suggest for guild invite**, or type `/gsuggest`, `/gsuggest First Surname` or `/gsuggest %t` for your target.

Members whose rank can invite see every suggestion on the **To decide** tab, with who suggested it, when, and why. WoW only lets its own interface send guild invites, so **Invite** puts the player's name in chat as a link: right-click it and pick **Guild Invite**, or type `/ginvite`. The suggestion is marked invited once the game confirms the invite went out, however you sent it. **Decline** asks first. The member who made the suggestion sees whether the player was invited or not, but not who decided.

The **Log** tab, also only for members who can invite, keeps every step with its date and time: who suggested whom, who invited or declined them, and who took a decided suggestion off the list. Nothing is ever dropped from it.

### Reasons stay private

A reason is only ever sent to members whose guild rank can invite (the guild master always can), and nothing about a suggestion is posted in guild chat. Members who can invite keep each other's lists in step, so whoever logs in next is up to date.

### Good to know

Addons can only talk to players who are online, and there is no server behind this addon. A suggestion waits on your side until someone who can invite is online at the same time as you, then goes out by itself. Everyone involved needs the addon: the member making a suggestion and the members who can invite.
