# GuildSuggestion

A small WoW Forever addon for guilds that keep invite rights to a few ranks. Any member can suggest a player to invite, with a short reason. Members who can invite get the suggestion, read the reason and choose Invite or Decline. The member who suggested it sees what happened, but not who decided.

Reasons stay private. They are only whispered to members whose guild rank can invite (the guild master always can), and those members keep each other's lists in step. Nothing about a suggestion is ever sent on the guild channel. If nobody who can invite is online, the suggestion waits and goes out when one of them logs in while you are online.

Open the guild window and pick the Guild suggestions tab under the game's own tabs, or type `/gsuggest`. You can also right-click a player (target frame, party, chat name) and pick "Suggest for guild invite", or type `/gsuggest First Surname` or `/gsuggest %t` for your target.

Everyone involved needs the addon: the member making a suggestion and the members who can invite.
